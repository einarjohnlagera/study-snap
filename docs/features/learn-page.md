# learn-page.md - NoteLib Feature Context

## Goal

The Learn page should work as:

- SEO content hub
- onboarding content hub
- public study-guide library

It should help visitors understand how to use NoteLib and how to study with reviewers, practice questions, weak concepts, and adaptive practice.

## Product Positioning

`NoteLib helps students, board exam reviewees, teachers, and professionals turn notes into summaries, key concepts, quizzes, and interview prep so they can study and prepare faster.`

## Learn Hub Structure

The Learn hub should group guides into five categories:

1. `For Students`
2. `For Board Exams`
3. `For Teachers`
4. `For Professionals`
5. `Study Tips`

Each category should contain guide cards with:

- title
- short description
- `Read Guide` action

## Guide Categories

### For Students

- `How to Turn Notes Into Reviewers`
- `How to Study Using Practice Questions`
- `How to Review Faster for Exams`

### For Board Exams

- `How to Use NoteLib for Board Exam Review`
- `How to Turn Reviewers Into Practice Questions`
- `How to Find Weak Areas Before the Board Exam`

### For Teachers

- `How Teachers Can Use NoteLib to Generate Study Packs`
- `How to Turn Lesson Notes Into Quiz Questions`
- `How to Create Reviewer Materials Faster`

### For Professionals

- `How to Prepare for Job Interviews With Your Notes`
- `How to Use AI Critique in Interview Practice`
- `How to Read Your Interview Readiness Report`

### Study Tips

- `What is Active Recall`
- `Why Practice Questions Are Effective`
- `How to Study Smarter`

## Article Pages

Article pages should reuse one shared public reading layout:

- H1 title
- short introduction
- summary section
- key concepts section
- practice questions / sample quiz section
- section headings
- mobile-friendly reading width
- CTA at the bottom linking to signup

## Article Structure

Each public Learn article should contain:

1. Topic title
2. Short introduction
3. Summary
4. Key concepts
5. Practice questions (sample quiz)
6. CTA

Bottom CTA copy should stay aligned with product marketing:

- title: `Want to turn your own notes into summaries and quizzes?`
- supporting line: `Try NoteLib for free`
- primary action: `Create Free Account`

## Messaging Rules

Use outcome-driven language:

- turn notes into quizzes
- reviewer
- practice questions
- weak concepts
- adaptive practice
- board exam review

Do not lead with technical terms like AI, OCR, LLM, or SaaS.

## Content Marketing Rule

- Learn pages should be useful even without login.
- Learn pages should work as public study resources that can rank in search and introduce students to NoteLib.
- Learn pages should end with a clear account-creation CTA, but the article body must still provide standalone value on its own.
