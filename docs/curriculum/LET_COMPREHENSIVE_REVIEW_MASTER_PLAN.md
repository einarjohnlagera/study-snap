## Important file-format note

This revision replaces the previous TSV block.

The earlier Markdown file was passed through Pandoc, which normalized
literal tab characters into spaces. That made `section`, `note_title`,
and `note_subject` ambiguous on many rows.

**The authoritative machine-readable block below is now CSV, not TSV.**
Every field is double-quoted. Parse it with a standard CSV parser; do
not split on whitespace.

## Strategic decision

This rebuild is aligned to the current PRC LEPT structure rather than
preserving the old four-bucket NoteLib structure.

1.  🌐 General Education
2.  🏫 Teaching Profession
3.  🎯 Curriculum, Teaching Strategies, and Educational Technology
4.  🧠 Child and Adolescent Learners and Learning Principles
5.  📝 Assessment of Learning
6.  🔎 Field Study, Teaching Internship, and Action Research

Secondary specialization reviewers should be separate reusable Review
Sets layered on top of this shared core. They are deliberately not mixed
into this master set.

## Subject Plan descriptions

### 1. 🌐 General Education

Build the broad intellectual, civic, cultural, scientific, mathematical,
and personal competencies assessed in the LEPT General Education
component, with emphasis on communication, reasoning, Philippine
society, ethical judgment, global awareness, and practical application.

### 2. 🏫 Teaching Profession

Understand the philosophical, sociological, historical, legal, ethical,
and professional foundations of teaching, including the Philippine
educational system, teacher professionalization, professional standards,
rights and responsibilities, and the teacher's role in school and
community.

### 3. 🎯 Curriculum, Teaching Strategies, and Educational Technology

Master curriculum planning and implementation, instructional design,
learner-centered teaching strategies, classroom management, new
literacies, and educational technology for effective, inclusive, and
developmentally appropriate teaching.

### 4. 🧠 Child and Adolescent Learners and Learning Principles

Understand child and adolescent development, learning theories,
motivation, cognition, individual differences, learner diversity,
guidance, and developmentally appropriate approaches for responsive and
inclusive teaching.

### 5. 📝 Assessment of Learning

Master the principles, construction, administration, interpretation, and
improvement of classroom assessment, including traditional and authentic
tools, validity and reliability, item analysis, educational statistics,
feedback, and assessment for learning.

### 6. 🔎 Field Study, Teaching Internship, and Action Research

Connect professional knowledge to authentic school practice through
observation, teaching assistantship, mentored classroom teaching,
portfolio development, reflective practice, school-community engagement,
and classroom-based action research.
