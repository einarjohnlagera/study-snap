# NoteLib Marketing Strategy

Last Updated: June 2026

---

# Purpose

This document captures the current marketing strategy for NoteLib.

The strategy is based on real engagement and experiments across:

* Facebook Page
* LET groups
* PNLE groups
* ALE groups

This document should be treated as the current source of truth.

---

# Core Marketing Principle

People do not come looking for NoteLib.

People come looking for:

* reviewer questions
* educational content
* trivia
* board exam preparation

Therefore:

Content first.

Product second.

---

# Positioning

NoteLib is not positioned as:

* an AI tool
* a note summarizer
* a NotebookLM competitor

Instead:

NoteLib is a study workflow.

Tagline:

"Your notes become your study system."

Core Loop:

Capture → Generate → Review → Improve → Copy → Repeat

---

# Audience Priority

Current priority audiences:

1. Architecture (ALE)
2. LET
3. PNLE

Reason:

These currently produce the strongest engagement and discussion.

---

# Facebook Page Strategy

Purpose:

* awareness
* authority
* educational discovery

---

## Content Type

Post:

* educational trivia
* science
* history
* board exam related concepts

Examples:

* Roman Architecture
* How the Light Bulb Works
* Why We Feel Nauseous After Exercise
* Inflation and Purchasing Power

---

## Image Rules

Image should contain:

* title only
* relevant image

Image should NOT contain:

* explanations
* bullet points
* infographic style content
* large blocks of text

The post contains the explanation.

The image creates curiosity.

---

## Posting Frequency

2 posts per day

Recommended:

* 9 AM
* 7 PM

---

# Success Metric

Primary:

* shares
* comments

Secondary:

* reactions

Tertiary:

* clicks

Reason:

Shares and comments indicate educational value.
