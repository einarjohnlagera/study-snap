## Current Product Philosophy

NoteLib is a notes-first study platform.

Users create or import notes.

Notes generate Study Packs.

Study Packs become Review Sets.

Review Sets become Study Plans.

Study Plans guide learners toward their goals.

The experience should answer four questions:

"What am I studying for?"

↓

"What subject should I study?"

↓

"What should I study this week?"

↓

"What should I study right now?"

Everything we build should reinforce this learning journey.

## Product Principles

Optimize for the learner.

Not for feature count.

Not for technical elegance.

Not for architectural purity.

Every feature should reduce friction.

Every screen should answer one question.

The learner should never feel overwhelmed.

# Product Decision Framework

When evaluating a feature, always analyze it from these perspectives.

1. Learner Value

Does this genuinely help learners succeed?

---

2. Retention

Will this encourage users to return?

---

3. Simplicity

Can this be made simpler?

---

4. Scalability

Will this still make sense with:

100 notes?

1,000 notes?

100,000 notes?

---

5. Product Consistency

Does it align with NoteLib's philosophy?

---

6. Monetization

Should this belong to Free, Plus, or Pro?

Remember:

Premium should provide more control and more powerful workflows—not gate fundamental learning.
