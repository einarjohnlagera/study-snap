# B7 Phase 1 — low reasoning effort on Luna: blind quality review results

**Status: COMPLETE. Result: low effort is NOT adopted.** The pre-stated rule fails on one criterion. That
criterion fails identically for default effort, so the failure is not attributable to low effort. **The
review also found a Luna answer-key defect pattern that matters for the v0.174.0 switch** (§3).

Written 2026-10-19 by Release Implementor for owner-approved B7 Phase 1
(`docs/claude-plans/2026-10-19-v0175-b7-low-effort-quality-review-proposal.md`).
- **No OpenAI calls.** Raw items, scores and the key are local and git-ignored, because they contain note
  text: `backend/target/llm-benchmark/b7/`; the key is kept outside that folder.

## Method

- **Questions:** 300 Luna questions from the targeted benchmark, 150 at default effort and 150 at
  `reasoning.effort: "low"`. All are real 15-question `hard` Challenge starts on the **same 10 Study Packs**,
  so the two arms are paired.
- **Blinding:** labels removed, items shuffled (seed 175), checked for leaked labels, and the key moved out
  of the readers' folder.
- **Scoring:** three fresh Opus agents scored 100 items each, seeing only the source notes and the items.
  Each item was scored 1–5 on factual, single best answer, distractors, explanation, faithfulness, clarity and
  difficulty, plus whether the key is correct and a defect flag. Every numeric item was worked by hand.
- **Re-check:** a fourth fresh agent independently re-checked all 9 flagged items. It confirmed **7 wrong
  keys, every one harmful**, and cleared 2: one key is correct with a defensible alternative, the other is a
  disputed premise taken from the source note.

**Against the criteria stated before scoring:**

| Criterion | Result |
|---|---|

- **The pre-stated rule says "failing any criterion means low effort is rejected for now", so it is
  rejected.** It is not re-scored after the fact.
- On every comparative measure, low effort matched default within noise.
- To pursue low effort further, the owner could amend criterion 4 to a relative form ("no worse than
  default") and approve Phase 2, about $0.40, with fresh items scored under the amended rule.

## 3. The bigger finding: Luna's "which statement is true" items

- **6 of the 26 statement-pair items (23%) had a wrong key**: 3 at default effort (b7-139, b7-215,
  b7-258) and 3 at low (b7-145, b7-183, b7-284).
- **The 7th wrong key (b7-103, default) is a single-answer clinical item**, also with its explanation
  contradicting the key.
- **Across the other 273 items there were no wrong keys.**
- **In 6 of the 7, the item's own explanation supports the right answer and contradicts the key.** The
  model reasons correctly and then writes the wrong option index.
- **The existing H4 check did not catch them.** H4 is `isAnswerExplanationInternallyInconsistent`, which
  runs with a retry in the generation path.
- **This affects v0.174.0.** After the owner's switch, FREE Study Pack quizzes are generated by Luna, and the
  statement-pair format can appear there too. The first benchmark's blind tier (20 items per model) was too
  small to see a pattern in one format.
- **Unknown, and needed before judging Luna:** whether today's `gpt-4.1-mini`, and Sol, make the same error
  at the same rate.
  - The targeted benchmark already saved 150 mini questions on the same packs (`e-*-mini`). A blind review
    of those costs nothing.
  - Sol's 150 questions on the same packs (`e-*-sol`) can be scored the same way.
- **Possible fix** (a proposal, not scoped): extend the consistency check to statement-pair items, testing
  whether the keyed option's truth pattern matches the explanation's per-statement verdicts, and retry on a
  mismatch.

## Other observations (both arms)

- **Difficulty is weak for `hard` on Luna:** mean 2.56. Many distractors are strawmen or absolutes, and some
  stems give the answer away.
- **Explanations score lower (about 3.6)** than other dimensions.
- **Near-duplicate items within a start are common** (for example, the same latitude/departure problem
  twice).
- These are quality issues with Luna's Challenge generation at any effort level, and inputs for v0.175.0
  Challenge routing (item 17).
