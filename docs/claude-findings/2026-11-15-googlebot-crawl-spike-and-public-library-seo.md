## A. Executive summary (plain language)

- **Google really did start crawling much harder, but our servers coped.** Googlebot's page (HTML) requests went from at most 16 a day to 200 on Nov 11, 903 on Nov 12 and 1,021 on Nov 13 (Search Console). On those days the database did not crash, its CPU never went above about 22% of its 0.1-core limit in any hour (most hours about 10%; the October crash hours ran 50 to 80%), and the backend's hourly request peaks on Nov 12 to 14 (1,041 / 852 / 486) were no higher than on Nov 10, the day before the crawl began (1,048). Google made 3,995 requests that day, yet the backend's volume did not rise above normal, so they cannot all have reached it.
- **The one recent database crash (Nov 11, 07:48 UTC) was not Googlebot.** It came in the middle of a two-hour burst that read more than 1,200 different public notes in a single hour. By Search Console's own day boundaries that hour belongs to Nov 10, when Google made 20 requests in total. Who caused it is unknown: our logs show only the website's own server as the caller.
- **Why Google started: not proven.** The timing fits our Nov 6 change that moved every canonical link and sitemap address from `notelib.app` to `www.notelib.app` (search engines re-check pages after such a change; 85% of this crawling is "refresh"), and also fits the Nov 11 release. I cannot separate them.
- **A side effect worth fixing:** every Googlebot visit to a note page counts as a "view" in our analytics, and views feed the Explore popularity ranking and creators' impact numbers. Bots are inflating both.
- **Search today:** 82% of search clicks are people typing the brand name. Public note pages get impressions (2,792 in three months) but only 23 clicks. Only 64 pages are indexed against 2,337 in the sitemap.
- **One privacy check is due:** the links people use to share notes and quizzes (`/shared/...`, `/p/...`, `/quiz/...`) are not blocked from search engines. I found no sign any is indexed, but nothing prevents it.

## C. Backend risk assessment

- **High-cost routes (code and statistics):** public note page (uncached render, then slug-resolution query, view-event insert, and rail lookups cached 120 s), filtered listings (`?courseProgram=`, `?subject=`), creator and profile pages (the public-profile metrics aggregate), and `/sitemap.xml` (547 KB; the walk is capped at once an hour per server instance).
- **Googlebot's own load looks manageable on the evidence above.** The bigger exposure is bursts like Nov 11 from an unidentified caller, plus the fixed-cost heavy queries in B7 that run regardless of Googlebot.
- **Unresolved evidence gaps:** who generated the Nov 11 burst; which callers drive the public-profile metrics and listing queries (public creator and profile pages, or crawlers); whether bursts originate at the Vercel edge (Hobby plan keeps runtime logs for one hour, so a past burst cannot be read back); the Vercel firewall and bot settings; what the 52% "Other file type" in Search Console's file-type split actually is.
- **Correlation caution:** the Nov 12–14 spike had no kill, and the Nov 11 kill had a different cause, so there is no evidence here that Googlebot itself endangered the database. It does not prove it cannot, since the database has no headroom (`basic_256mb`).

## D. SEO findings

**Technical indexing**
1. **Canonicals and hosts are sound.** Apex 308 to `www`; note pages self-canonical; tag URLs canonical to `/public/library` (`x-vercel-cache: HIT`); not-found notes are `noindex`; subject pages with fewer than 6 notes are `noindex` and left out of the sitemap (`SUBJECT_PAGE_INDEX_THRESHOLD = 6`). Google's crawl is already almost entirely on `www` (11,168 requests against 71 on the apex), but Search Console still shows `https://notelib.app/public/library/pharmacology` with impressions, so some old-host results remain.
2. **Facet URLs are crawl waste, not a backend problem.** 258 of the 712 example URLs Google lists (36%; Google's examples, not a random sample) are listing variants: 234 `?tag=`, 9 `?courseProgram=`, 8 `?subject=`, 7 `?creator=`. All canonicalize away; the tag pages are cached shells. Note pages link to them (`app/public/library/[subject]/[slug]/page.tsx:224, 377, 462`), which is how they are discovered.
3. **Sitemap:** 2,337 URLs, all `www` (2,186 notes, 121 subject pages, 30 other), `lastmod` real (2,307 present, 21 within the last 7 days), so no refresh-inflation from fake dates. Only **64 pages are indexed**, so either Google has not got to the rest yet or indexing is limited; I could not tell whether the sitemap was submitted in Search Console.
4. **Legacy URLs:** `/public/notes/{id}` redirects to the SEO URL with `redirect()` (a temporary 307), and `/notes/public/{id}` makes a second hop to it (`app/public/notes/[id]/page.tsx:15`, `app/notes/public/[id]/page.tsx:11`). `/{id}/view` is **not a route in this app**: it returns the JSON `{"code":"not_found","message":"Not found"}` with 404 (an unknown path elsewhere returns an HTML 404), yet Search Console recorded `OK (200)` for it at 3:17 PM on Nov 14. Origin unexplained.
5. **Structured data and metadata:** Article and BreadcrumbList JSON-LD, per-note title, description and social image on note pages. No problem found.
6. **Privacy (verify):** `/shared/notes/[id]`, `/shared/study-packs/[id]`, `/p/[token]` and `/quiz/[token]` are client pages with no `noindex` metadata, and `robots.txt` (disallows only `/dashboard`, `/study`, `/settings`, `/profile`, `/api`, `/app`) does not cover them. They are not in the sitemap and I found no evidence any is indexed; the exposure requires an outside link. Auth-gated app pages (`/library`, `/collections`, `/notes`) redirect to login. Note: Search Console reports "7 excluded by noindex", which URLs those are was not in the data I had.

**Content and conversion**
- **Brand dominates:** 5 brand-style queries hold 68 of 83 query clicks (82%); homepage 102 clicks of 2,348 impressions.
- **Note pages:** 164 pages with impressions, 2,792 impressions, 23 clicks (0.8%). Examples: reflection on action/in action 726 impressions, 3 clicks, position 10.9; models and levels of reflective practice 499 impressions, position 38.8; high-alert medications 139 impressions, position 47; query "thomasites" 84 impressions at position 5.6 with 0 clicks; "pharmacology reviewer" 61 impressions, 4 clicks. 14 note pages have 20 or more impressions and no clicks.
- **Audience:** Philippines 44 clicks of 727 impressions; Nepal 1,080 impressions for 12 clicks; US 999 for 17. Mobile has 68 of 132 clicks.
- **Landing experience:** the call to action ("Ready to quiz yourself?", free account) sits below the note and a mini-quiz preview, and only shows when the note has a quiz and is not the viewer's own (`page.tsx:300-316`). Breadcrumbs, related notes, subject and exam-hub links exist. Signups with a recorded first-touch surface in October: landing 5, public note 4, explore 2; November so far: landing 3, exam hub 1.
