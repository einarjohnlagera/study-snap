## 1. The answer first: the premise is mostly already true

**Automatic pool creation is already off by default** (`exam-pool-prewarm-enabled: ${EXAM_POOL_PREWARM_ENABLED:false}`, `application.yaml:297`; `StudySnapProperties.java:246`, CONFIRMED). **But pool generation is not on-demand-only.** The flag gates one thing: the eager `initiatePool` call at Study Pack generation (`ExamQuestionPoolService.java:230`). Every other trigger writes or regenerates pools without checking it, and some run when no learner asked. The production value of the flag is unread (an owner screen read; the cost plan's M0.3).

So the question is not "turn prewarm off". It is "stop the ungated paths that spend when nobody asked, and keep what a learner's real request needs." The learner experience for a missing pool is also **already on-demand** (section 3). That makes the proposal small.

## 3. What a learner sees today, and what would change

- **Today (flag off), for a Pro learner starting an exam on a note with no pool:** a `GENERATING` session, polling, a progress overlay and resume support; about 20 to 37 s for Long and 10 to 23 s for Board in the sampled production logs (QUOTED from the v0.182.0 sequencing doc), with a two-in-ten schema-or-parse failure rate seen on Long Exam in that small sample (it recovered on a second attempt). No pool UI exists or is needed.
- **If the regeneration-time refresh were removed (section 6):** the only visible effect is that the **first exam after a pack regeneration is generated on demand instead of served instantly from a refreshed pool**. That is exactly the already-supported on-demand path. Free and Plus: no effect. Plan, Review Set and multi-note exams: no effect (never pooled).
- **Removing automatic creation entirely is not proposed.** Without any pool, every exam would be generated on demand, and Long and Board exams that today start instantly from a ready pool would each wait for generation.

## 4. Estimated financial benefit (MODELED; assumptions stated; no production counts yet)

**Cost per pool fill, at the `INTERACTIVE_QUIZ` default `gpt-4.1-mini` ($0.40 in / $1.60 out per million tokens, `StudySnapProperties.java:605-610`):**
- **Long pool** (48 questions, two parallel batches of 26, `PARALLEL_BUFFER=2`; *auditor-reported*): at the benchmark's assumed size of about 3,400 in and 3,600 out per batch, **about $0.014** per fill. **The benchmark prices a pool batch like a normal Long batch; a pool batch is 26 questions against about 15 per logged Long batch (a figure derived from `ceil(25/2)+2`, not stated in any source doc), so real output is probably about 1.7 times higher, about $0.023.** The logged Long output is about 3,300 tokens, not 3,600 (cost-ceilings doc, release branch). ESTIMATED range **$0.014 to $0.023**.
- **Board pool** (24 questions, two sequential 12-question calls): about 1,750 in and 1,800 out per call (logged), **about $0.007** per fill. ESTIMATED.
- **Both pools of one pack: about $0.021 to $0.030, before retries and failures** (the unmeasured overhead; the sampled Long parse-failure rate suggests it is not zero). If the owner moves these routes to `gpt-6-luna` under `v0.182.0` ($0.10 / $0.50), the figure falls to roughly **0.3 times** (output-heavy; corrected by the cold review from "a quarter").

**This is a GROSS figure, not the saving (cold review, CONFIRMED by reading `sampleQuestions` and `generatePoolAsync`).** Today, an exam that follows a regeneration is served from the refreshed pool with no LLM call. Under B1 the same exam pays for an on-demand session **and** a pool fill (a missing row triggers lazy creation; a FAILED row is refreshed and the sample returns empty). So B1 **defers** the pool cost to the next exam request instead of removing it. With *C_pool* the cost of the pools that exist, *C_session* the cost of one on-demand session (about $0.014 for Long at two batches, about $0.004 for Board at one 12-question call, ESTIMATED at `gpt-4.1-mini`) and *p* the share of regenerations that are followed by an exam before the next regeneration: **net saving per regeneration ≈ C_pool × (1 − p) − C_session × p**. It is zero for a pack with no pool row, and **it is negative when most regenerations are followed by an exam**. The saving exists only for regenerations **never followed by an exam**, which is the common case for **admin-curated packs** (their pools serve only the admin account, section 2 row 4) and the rare case for an active learner. *p* has not been read (section 5).

**Monthly bound (MODELED upper bound, not a forecast):** the cost audit reports 653 admin-owned packs generated in August (QUOTED). If every one were an in-place regeneration of a pack with both pools, the spend would be about 653 × $0.021 to $0.030, **about $14 to $20 a month**; October's 164 admin packs would be about $3.50 to $4.90. **The real figure is lower, because first-time generation does not refresh and a pack needs an existing pool.** What decides it is the count of regenerations of packs that have pools (section 5), which has not been read.

**Two things the dollar figure should not hide:**
1. **This spend is invisible to the existing cost alert.** `CurationCostAlertJob` sums only `study_packs` tokens for ADMIN-owned packs; pool fills are never counted (CONFIRMED in the cost plan, section 2).
2. **It scales with learners.** When Pro learners regenerate their own packs, each regeneration refreshes their pools at our cost, unrequested. At the current ₱149 Pro price (about $2.6 gross at an illustrative ₱58 per dollar) a few cents per regeneration is small but not zero.

**Honest summary (corrected):** the monthly bound above is the **gross** spend on regeneration-time refreshes; the net saving is smaller and concentrated in regenerations that no exam follows, mainly **admin-curated packs**. At today's scale it is **small** (a few dollars to tens of dollars a month at most, likely less). The case for a change rests on removing an invisible cost line and on not paying for unrequested work as learners arrive, **not** on a large saving, and a blanket B1 could cost more than it saves once learners regenerate packs they then examine.

### 4b. The larger unrequested cost is the Long Exam depletion refill (cold review, CONFIRMED)
After every completed pooled Long Exam, `markServed` refreshes the **whole 48-question pool** whenever fewer than `longExamHighTierCount` = 30 questions remain (`ExamQuestionPoolService.java:131-139`, `examSizeForMode` at `:427-433`, `StudySnapProperties:235-236`). Sessions take 20, 25 or 30 questions (`:273-278`), leaving 28, 23 or 18, **all under 30**, so **every pooled Long Exam triggers a full refill**. Questions are marked served at sample time, so at 25 or 30 questions one fill serves exactly one exam. A Long pool therefore generates about **48 questions per exam served, against about 30 to 34 on demand** (`batchSize = ceil(n/2)+2`, `OpenAiLlmStudyPackService:2191`; auditor-reported). **For Long Exam the pool is a latency feature, not a cost saver, and the refill is recurring unrequested generation** that also queues on the 2-thread executor. Board is roughly cost-neutral (24 questions serve two 12-question exams; auditor-reported). This is the strongest candidate for a change that matches Product UX's direction, and it is **not** in B1.

## 6. Minimal implementation proposal (PROPOSAL; technically feasible; one backend change; no new infrastructure)

**Principle:** pools are created or rebuilt only when a learner's own exam request needs one, never as a side effect of regenerating a pack. Keep everything that already responds to demand.

- **B1 (corrected): make regeneration invalidate, not regenerate, and scope it to where it pays.** At the three regeneration call sites (`StudyPackService:918-921`, `AdminStudyPackTransactionHelper:80-81`, `:148-149`) replace "reset to PENDING and dispatch generation" with "invalidate the pool without dispatching": the next exam request then follows the existing on-demand path. **Because B1 defers rather than removes the cost (section 4), the net saving is positive only where regenerations are rarely followed by an exam. The narrowest version, recommended as the first candidate, applies it only to packs owned by an ADMIN** (their pools serve only the admin account, section 2 row 4): bulk curation regenerations then stop refreshing pools, and learner packs keep today's behaviour until *p* for learners is known.
  - **Option (a) delete the pool row (SUPERSEDED by Product UX, section 12: no automatic deletion of existing pools).** CONFIRMED safe on the in-flight case: `generatePoolAsync` loads the row with `orElseThrow` (`ExamQuestionPoolService:152, :194`), the catch at `:212-220` only logs and its `ifPresent` does nothing, so no NPE and no orphan write; the in-flight work is lost but was already paid for. `UNIQUE(study_pack_id, mode)` makes the next lazy creation clean. **Cost of (a):** lazy creation then runs inside the learner's exam-start transaction (`scheduleLazyInitiation` calls `initiatePoolForUsage` on `this`, bypassing the `NOT_SUPPORTED` proxy; auditor-reported `AppConfig:282-286`), so an insert failure rolls back the exam start, and that exposure grows from "first exam ever" to "first exam after every regeneration". The risk is low (starts are serialized by the `study_packs` FOR UPDATE lock), but the brief must test it.
  - **Option (b) mark the row non-servable, reusing FAILED.** **No migration needed: V58 has `CHECK (generation_status IN ('PENDING','GENERATING','READY','FAILED'))` (CONFIRMED); a new status would need one.** **But (b) must also bump `generationStatusAt`**: `generatePoolAsync` discards a result only when that stamp changed (CONFIRMED `:195`), and a task already GENERATING has captured the old pack content (`:164`), so without the bump it would write READY with old-content questions, a **stale pool served**. `GenerationRecoveryRowWriter:218-219` already bumps it and is the pattern to copy. (b) also cannot cancel a queued PENDING task, which proceeds and spends (`:153-156`), reading the by-then fresh pack, so no stale data but unrequested spend. After (b), the next request refreshes the pool immediately and returns empty, so that exam pays a session **and** a pool fill, as under (a).
  - Preserve the lock order (`study_packs` then `exam_question_pool`): keep the invalidation after the existing `flush()` (`StudyPackService:914`; `AdminStudyPackTransactionHelper:79, :147`).
- **B2: stop a second paid generation when a refresh lands on a PENDING or GENERATING row** (a small guard, `generatePoolAsync` skips only READY today). Probably moot once B1 removes the regeneration callers; the brief confirms which callers remain.
- **B3 (new candidate, owner decision): the Long depletion refill (section 4b).** Today every completed pooled Long Exam queues a full 48-question refill. Options: keep it (instant next exam, recurring unrequested generation); or refill only when a request actually finds too few questions (the next exam is generated on demand and then refills), which removes the recurring spend at the cost of one slower exam per pool. Not proposed as a default; it needs the owner's call and the *p* and pooled-Long-exam counts first.
- **Keep, no change proposed:** the lazy creation on a first miss; the retry of a FAILED pool on the next start. **Open question for the owner (section 8):** whether the lazy pool fill beside a first exam is worth its cost (a one-time user pays for a pool they never use), since the alternative is to build the pool only on a second request.
- **Not proposed:** predictive prewarming, scheduling, new infrastructure, a new learner-facing pool status, any change to routing, prompts, models, quotas, entitlements or generation behaviour for plan, Review Set or multi-note exams.
- **Size:** S, backend only, a few call sites plus tests. **Existing tests that pin the old refresh behaviour and would change (auditor-reported):** `StudyPackServiceTest:1181-1183`, `:1666-1668`; `AdminStudyPackTransactionHelperTest:170-178`, `:227-232`; `ExamQuestionPoolServiceTest:376-395` if `refreshPool` itself is touched. **Deploy shape:** a Render deploy, so its own approval, the section-5 checklist and at least 24 hours from any other deploy; it competes for slots with `v0.182.0` (whose route switches are Render saves that also redeploy) and must not displace it. It does not touch routing or prompts, but Long Exam and its pool are in the migration's scope, so **sequence it after the migration's production switches unless the owner decides otherwise**, to keep the migration's benchmark and pilot comparisons uncontaminated.

## 7. Risks to the first-time Pro exam experience

1. **Every first exam on a note waits for generation (about 20 to 37 s for Long).** This is true today with the flag off; B1 only extends it to the first exam after a regeneration. It must never be described as instant: copy must say the exam is being prepared and can be resumed.
2. **Reliability of the first run.** A small production sample showed 2 of 10 Long Exam calls failing parse and recovering on a second attempt. A failed first generation for the first paying learner is the worst case; refunds exist (the session fails with a quota refund) but the experience must read as a calm retry. This is also the reason a later, evidence-based selective prewarm may be justified for Official notes; **but prewarming an Official source pack helps no adopter, because a copy gets its own pack and its own pool** (section 2, row 4).
3. **A first miss starts two paid fills on the same 2-thread executor** (the session and the lazy pool), so a busy moment can queue the learner's own session behind a pool fill (*auditor-reported*, executor behaviour under load unverified).
4. **A depleted READY pool is not refreshed by `sampleQuestions`** (*auditor-reported*, unconfirmed at runtime): an abandoned first pool-sourced exam never calls `markServed`, so such a pack falls back to on-demand generation each time until it is regenerated. It costs the same as having no pool, but should be understood before judging pool hit rates.
5. **Stale pool risk if B1 is done wrongly:** invalidating must guarantee a regenerated pack never serves its old pool (the learner-level and answer-key filters are not a substitute); **option (b) without a `generationStatusAt` bump serves a stale pool (section 6)**.
6. **Under B1 the first exam after a regeneration also starts a pool fill** (a missing row triggers lazy creation), so that exam pays a session and a pool fill on the shared executor, which is the same pairing a first exam has today.
7. **Today every completed pooled Long Exam queues a 48-question refill** on the same 2-thread executor, competing with on-demand sessions (section 4b).

## 8. Acceptance criteria (for a later brief; not approved)

- **Pool reuse:** a valid, READY pool is served immediately (`IN_PROGRESS` session, `poolSourced` marked) with no LLM call.
- **No unrequested generation:** regenerating a Study Pack that has Long and Board pools makes **zero** pool LLM calls (assert on the LLM client, not on a return value), for learner and admin paths and for admin summary regeneration and repair.
- **On-demand after invalidation:** the next exam request on that pack produces a `GENERATING` session, the existing polling and resume behaviour, and a rebuilt pool; the session is not marked `poolSourced`.
- **Freshness:** a regenerated pack never serves a question from its previous pool; a note edited without regeneration keeps its pool (the existing rule).
- **Status:** a learner who leaves during generation can return to the same session (both modes); the copy never promises instant availability.
- **No duplicate generation:** two overlapping triggers for one pack and mode result in one paid generation; a double click returns the same session.
- **Entitlements and quotas unchanged:** Long and Board stay Pro-only; Free and Plus never create pools; pool-served and generated sessions charge the same units; a failed generation refunds the unit (existing `failLongExamSession` and `failBoardExamSession`); pool fills charge no quota.
- **Failure recovery:** a failed pool fill leaves the pack usable (the next request retries); stale pending sessions and pools still recover on their existing timers.
- **Scope:** plan, Review Set and multi-note exams behave exactly as before; adopters' copies and Official source packs are unaffected; no change to routing, prompts, models or providers.
- **Real requests:** MockMvc or service-level tests that exercise the real regeneration and exam-start paths, plus a PostgreSQL test for the row lock and the unique key. Existing tests that pin the old refresh behaviour change and are listed in the brief.

## 9. Recommendation and the decisions that are genuinely the owner's

**Does it warrant its own implementation brief? Conditionally, yes, but not urgently, and the scope should be narrower than first drafted.** B1 is a small, contained, independent backend change that removes an invisible cost and a duplicate-generation hole, and it matches Product UX's direction; **but it defers the pool cost rather than removing it, so it should start with admin-owned packs**, and the **Long depletion refill (B3) is the larger recurring unrequested spend**. At today's scale the dollars are small, so: **run the section 5 reads first (especially *p* and the pooled-Long-exam counts); if admin regenerations dominate or learners are about to arrive, write the brief; otherwise defer it behind the migration.** It must not be merged into Workstream A.

Genuine product or financial trade-offs for the owner (and Product UX where noted):
1. **Whether to approve B1 now or after the reads and the migration.** Recommended: after the reads (including *p*), sequenced after the migration's production switches, and **starting with the admin-owned-packs-only version**.
1b. **The Long depletion refill (B3). Settled in part by Product UX (12.9, Q2 A): stopped for team-owned packs only; the learner-owned refill stays deferred to the owner's counts.** Keep it (instant next exam) or move to refill-on-shortfall (cheaper, one slower exam per pool). The biggest recurring unrequested spend found, so it deserves a deliberate decision.
2. **Whether the lazy pool fill beside a first exam stays** (PRODUCT UX plus owner): keep (default, instant second exam) or build the pool only on the second request (cheaper, slower second start).
3. **What counts as demand evidence for any later selective prewarming** (for example pool-sourced hit rates per note or program): to be defined, with a spending ceiling, before any prewarm is considered. Not proposed now.
4. **Whether pools on admin-owned packs should be created at all** (an observation, not a proposal): they serve only the admin account, and admin testing of exams would simply use the on-demand path.

### 12.4 The minimum production reads for Q1 (SELECT only; owner or an attended read; run each separately, off-peak)
Column names *auditor-reported*; verify against the migrations first. `session_mode` and `status` are string-stored; Long Exam is `session_mode='LONG_EXAM'`; Board is `session_mode='CHALLENGE'` with `session_state->>'mode'='board_exam'`; a pooled session has `session_state->>'poolSourced'='true'`; completed means `status='COMPLETED' AND completed_at IS NOT NULL`.
```sql
-- Q1a pooled Long Exams completed, per month (each one triggers a full refill, section 4b)
SELECT date_trunc('month', completed_at) AS month,
       count(*) FILTER (WHERE session_state->>'poolSourced' = 'true') AS pooled_completed,
       count(*) AS all_long_completed
FROM quick_review_sessions
WHERE session_mode = 'LONG_EXAM' AND status = 'COMPLETED' AND completed_at IS NOT NULL
GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 1;
-- Q1b repeat Long Exams on the same note, and the gap between exams, by owner role
WITH s AS (
  SELECT user_id, study_pack_id, created_at,
         lag(created_at) OVER (PARTITION BY user_id, study_pack_id ORDER BY created_at) AS prev
  FROM quick_review_sessions
  WHERE session_mode = 'LONG_EXAM' AND status IN ('COMPLETED','IN_PROGRESS','PAUSED') AND study_pack_id IS NOT NULL)
SELECT u.role, count(*) AS sessions, count(*) FILTER (WHERE s.prev IS NOT NULL) AS repeat_sessions,
       percentile_cont(0.5) WITHIN GROUP (ORDER BY extract(epoch FROM (s.created_at - s.prev)) / 3600)
         FILTER (WHERE s.prev IS NOT NULL) AS median_gap_hours
FROM s JOIN users u ON u.id = s.user_id GROUP BY u.role;
-- Q1c READY pools whose current fill has not been used (served keys are reset to [] on every fill)
SELECT p.mode, COALESCE(u.role, 'NO_OWNER') AS owner_role, count(*) AS ready_unused_current_fill
FROM exam_question_pool p JOIN study_packs sp ON sp.id = p.study_pack_id LEFT JOIN users u ON u.id = sp.owner_user_id
WHERE p.generation_status = 'READY' AND jsonb_array_length(p.served_question_keys) = 0
GROUP BY p.mode, COALESCE(u.role, 'NO_OWNER');
-- Q1d pool census by mode, status and owner role
SELECT p.mode, p.generation_status, COALESCE(u.role, 'NO_OWNER') AS owner_role, count(*) AS pools, sum(p.pool_size) AS questions
FROM exam_question_pool p JOIN study_packs sp ON sp.id = p.study_pack_id LEFT JOIN users u ON u.id = sp.owner_user_id
GROUP BY p.mode, p.generation_status, COALESCE(u.role, 'NO_OWNER');
```
**Limits, stated up front (second cold review):** `study_packs.owner_user_id` is nullable, so Q1c and Q1d use a LEFT JOIN so such packs are not dropped; **served keys are marked at sampling time, so Q1c counts a pool whose sample was taken and abandoned as "used"**; Q1a covers Long Exam only (a Board equivalent would filter `session_mode='CHALLENGE'` and `session_state->>'mode'='board_exam'`). `refreshed_at` is overwritten on each fill and there is no history table, so Q1c shows only the **current** fill, not every past refill; there is no session-to-pool key (a join needs `study_pack_id`, the mode mapping and `created_at > refreshed_at`). **Estimated cost of refills = the Q1a pooled-completed count (each triggers one) multiplied by the per-fill cost in section 4** (about $0.014 to $0.023 Long, $0.007 Board), labelled ESTIMATED; the unused share comes from Q1c. These are aggregates over small tables; an earlier heavy production join in this project timed out, so keep each query separate and bounded.

### 12.13 Genuine contradictions with existing behaviour or approved requirements, and the separate reliability triage
**Contradictions and cautions found while settling the rulings (CONFIRMED unless marked):**
1. **The preparing overlay is a SHARED component** (`components/study-pack/quiz-generation-overlay.tsx`): it is also used by **Adaptive Practice** (`adaptive-practice/page.tsx:876`) and **Interview Practice** (`interview-practice/page.tsx:512`), not only Long and Board. **Its default helper text is the sentence to be removed, and an exit control would appear on those screens too.** For Adaptive and Interview, "You can return to this exam later" is **not verified** (their resume behaviour was not audited). **Therefore the exit control, the new copy and the removal of the default helper text must be opt-in per consumer (props), not a change to the shared default**, or they must be verified for all four screens first.
2. **"You can return to this exam later" is bounded.** A session still GENERATING after the recovery sweeper's bound (30 minutes plus up to a 10-minute cadence, both modes) is marked FAILED with a refund; a learner returning after that sees the failure state. Whether and how long a READY (IN_PROGRESS) session persists was not audited. The copy is true within the generation window; state the bound to the engineers.
3. **Leaving does not cancel generation or refund the allowance** (a pending session keeps its charge until it completes or the sweeper fails it); the exit control must not imply otherwise.
4. **The "stop the Long refill for team-owned packs" ruling and the deferred learner refill coexist** only because the owner test is on the pack owner; the tests must assert both sides.
5. **The role rule is not a definition of Official content** (Q1); wording in docs and release notes must not claim it is.
6. **No privacy or correctness requirement is contradicted**: pools are never public; the Study Pack is read-only during generation (CONFIRMED earlier), so "Your Study Pack is safe" is true.

**Separate reliability triage (outside both workstreams; recorded as Product UX asked; do not fix silently):**
- **R1: Long Exam quota accounting.** The Long allowance is charged after the session commits; a crash or exception in that gap leaves a session with `longExamQuotaReserved` set and no charge, and the recovery job then refunds a unit that was never charged (clamped at zero; it can credit another session's unit). CONFIRMED by the code's own comment (`LongExamService` around `:267-273`); the recovery path is auditor-reported (`GenerationRecoveryRowWriter` around `:281-289`).
- **R2: rejected generation dispatch.** A session saved without an active generation job (for example if the after-commit dispatch is rejected by the executor, whose queue is bounded at 100 with an abort policy) stays GENERATING until the recovery job fails it at 30 to 40 minutes. **Inferred, not tested: the severity is not assigned until it is verified** (Spring's after-commit exception behaviour under rejection).
- **Request:** a separate reliability assessment and prioritization through the established process (the Prod Investigator can verify R2 read-only; severity and any fix are the owner's call). Nothing in this plan fixes them.
