```text
Information Systems and Technology-Enabled Change in Enterprises

📘 Overview
Information systems and the technologies behind them change how enterprises operate, what they offer and how people work. These changes appear in four areas: operations, products, services and ways of working. Enterprises whose activities depend increasingly on digital technologies and information systems are often described as moving toward digital business.

🧠 Key Idea
Information systems do not only speed up existing work. They can change what work is done, how it is done and what an organization can offer. A change in one area usually brings changes in the others, so organizations plan the change as well as the technology.

⚔️ Core Details:
- Operations: systems can automate routine steps, connect separate activities and reduce handoffs between people. In a fictional regional bank, a system that checks routine loan applications against set rules replaces manual review of those cases and connects the branch to the loan department.
- Products: digital technology can add to or reshape what an organization offers. The bank offers a mobile wallet that its customers use to store and send money.
- Services: technology changes how services are delivered and reached. The bank lets customers open accounts and ask questions online, by phone or by chat instead of only at a branch.
- Ways of working: shared systems and remote access change where and how people work together. Bank staff can handle some customer requests from different locations and coordinate through shared systems.
- Digital business is the use of digital technologies and information systems to carry out an enterprise's operations, products and services. In a digital business, much of the organization's activity depends on its systems.
- The four areas are linked. Online account opening (a service) changes branch procedures (operations), and staff need new skills and routines (ways of working).
- Technology-enabled change is more than installing a system. The change succeeds when the organization alters its activities to use what the technology makes possible, and planning it means considering the people, processes and data affected as well as the technology.

🎯 Why It Matters:
- Seeing the four areas helps people notice the full effects of a new system, not only the technical ones.
- Because the areas are linked, a change planned in one area prompts questions about the others, such as which procedures or skills must change.
- Understanding digital business explains why many enterprises treat their information systems as part of how they operate and compete, not as a separate support function.
- Seeing change as planned and linked across areas prepares for the later topics on developing systems and on helping people adopt them.

🧠 Quick Recall:
- Technology-enabled change — a change in an enterprise's activities made possible by information systems and technology.
- Process automation — using a system to carry out routine steps that people used to do by hand.
- Digital service delivery — providing a service through digital channels such as online, mobile or chat.
- Digital business — carrying out an enterprise's operations, products and services largely through digital technologies and information systems.
- Ways of working — where, when and how people do their work together, which shared systems can change.
```
