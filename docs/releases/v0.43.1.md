# Release Notes: v0.43.1 — Companion Mentor Tips

## Release Theme

Let the *authored* Companion content participate in the Coach experience the way live signals already do — small, individually-surfaceable, action-linked "Mentor Tips" instead of an article to read start to finish — plus a pre-signoff tightening pass that fixed a real paywall trust bug and closed Help Center gaps for this cycle's features.

## Key Features

**💡 Mentor Tips**
- Curators can author small, individually-linkable tips on top of a Companion — each with its own identity, an optional linked action (Continue Studying, Review Due Concepts, or the terminal exam/builder action), and an optional deterministic surfacing condition (date- or progress-based).
- At most one eligible tip surfaces near Today's Focus, selected deterministically in authored order — no per-view LLM call, no learning-pattern inference.
- Every tip stays reachable under "View Full Guide" regardless of whether its surfacing condition is currently active — a learner never permanently misses a curator's tip.
- AI-assisted drafting extends to tips (title/body only); the linked action and surfacing condition are always curator-set, never model-set, preserving "Curation, never generation."
- Lives inside the existing Companion JSONB payload — no new table, migration, or endpoint.

**🔒 Paywall trust fix**
- Board Exam Mode, Long Exam, Difficulty Selection, and Interview Practice are all Pro-exclusive features. The upgrade paywall previously let a Free or Plus user select and check out for Plus from one of these paywalls and still not receive the feature. The Plus plan card is now correctly non-selectable on these Pro-only paywalls, with a checkout-time guard as defense in depth.

**❓ Help Center coverage for this cycle's features**
- New Learning Companion guide covering the Companion, Today's Focus, Mentor Tips, the collapsed Full Guide, and the official-author workflow.
- The Study Plans & Collections guide now also explains Primary Review Sets and target-date pacing.

## Polish & Fixes

- The Note Detail review-timing upgrade prompt now opens dedicated "See your review timing" paywall copy instead of the unrelated Adaptive Practice paywall.
- Fixed stale Help Center and `docs/features/collections.md` references to the removed "This Week" countdown card, caught during pre-signoff audit — weeks/concepts remaining now live in the Progress card, daily concept budget in Today's Focus's coaching sentence.
