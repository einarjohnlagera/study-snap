# Release Notes: v0.50.4 — Exam Hub Discovery Polish

## Release Theme

Make NoteLib's free reviewer content actually discoverable in search — rewrite Exam Hub and Public Library subject pages to speak the language students search for, add a defensive indexation gate so thin pages don't dilute that signal, and clean up a duplicate navigation link along the way.

## Key Features

**🔍 Public Library-Wide SEO Vocabulary Pass**
- Every Public Library subject page (not just the 3 curated Exam Hubs) now leads with "Free {Subject} reviewer notes and practice quizzes" in its title, meta description, and on-page heading — matching how students actually search, instead of generic library copy.
- Exam Hub pages (ALE, PNLE, LET) got the same treatment first, then the pass was extended app-wide after direct production testing showed NoteLib wasn't surfacing for exam-adjacent searches like "biology reviewer for grade school."

**🛡️ Subject-Page Indexation Gate**
- Subject pages with fewer than 6 notes are now excluded from the sitemap and marked `noindex` — they stay fully reachable and render normally in-app, they just stop being served to search engines while too thin to be a good result.
- Threshold (`SUBJECT_PAGE_INDEX_THRESHOLD = 6`) is grounded in a production content-depth inventory: 92 of 130 subject pages had fewer than 6 notes, and matches the existing `DISCOVERY_SECTION_LIMIT` constant rather than an arbitrary number.

## Polish & Fixes

- Collapsed the duplicate "Browse {Hub} hub →" link on public note detail pages down to a single link, pointing at the full course/program filter instead of repeating the callout banner's destination.
- Ran a broader SEO expansion Fable session on whether to extend curated Exam Hub treatment beyond ALE/PNLE/LET — verdict was "not now"; a CPALE (Wave 2 Exam Hub) candidate surfaced as a side-finding and is logged in the roadmap, unscoped.
- Logged the public-note copy idempotency guard's stale-data edge case (found while investigating what turned out to be a false-alarm regression report) as a low-priority backlog item — not fixed this release.

## Known limitations

None specific to this release.
