# Release Notes: v0.91.0 — Shared Learning Material

## Release Theme
You can finally give someone a note without publishing it to the world.

## Key Features

**📨 Share a note with the people you choose**
- Until now the only way to share a note was to make it **public** — the Share action told you so outright, so a parent helping one child had to publish that Study Pack into Explore for everyone
- A note can now be shared with specific people you are connected with, and it stays private the whole time
- You pick recipients one by one; nothing is ever shared with everyone you know just because you are connected to them
- Choosing **Private** again removes everyone's access, and tells you who is about to lose it before it happens

**📚 What they receive is the real thing, not a stripped-down copy**
- Shared notes arrive in their own **Shared with you** section of the Library, clearly marked with who sent them — never mixed in with your own notes
- Opening one gives the full Study Pack: summary, key ideas, full notes, then practice
- Their practice counts toward **their** progress. Your mastery, scores and history never travel with the material
- **Copy to my Library** keeps an independent copy that stays theirs even if you later stop sharing

**🔎 The product stopped hiding this from people**
- The landing page had been advertising supporter features as **"Coming Soon"** for three releases after they shipped, complete with a waitlist button — it now describes what actually exists
- The Help Center gained a **Helping Someone Learn** section covering quiz links, connections, sharing, and the privacy boundaries
- Both are written to be relationship-neutral: parents, tutors, siblings and study partners, with no implied "guardian mode"

**✍️ The invitation form behaves like part of the product**
- The birth-year field accepted letters and reported problems through the browser's own pop-up, which named no field and looked like nothing else in NoteLib
- It now takes four digits, offers a stepper that cannot step outside a real year, and flags an impossible year as you type it
- Errors appear beside the field that caused them, and there is deliberately **no pre-filled year** — that value is permanent and drives guardian consent, so it should never be something you tab past

## Polish & Fixes
- Selecting Private while the list of recipients was still loading could set a note private, skip the confirmation and quietly leave everyone's access intact
- Choosing "Share with connections" on a public note no longer unpublishes it without asking
- Finishing a Quick Review on shared material no longer offers links that lead to "this note does not belong to your account"
- "Copy to my Library" no longer creates a new duplicate every time it is pressed
- Sharing a note with someone you are connected to in both directions no longer fails outright
- All four birth-year inputs on the connections page are now the same control; one previously accepted anything at all
- The birth-year correction form validates in place instead of falling back to the browser's pop-up
- A Memorization error message was rendering in the ordinary text colour, so a failed grade did not look like a failure
- Confirmation copy now says "and 1 other" rather than "and 1 others"
