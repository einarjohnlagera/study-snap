# Release Notes: v0.72.0 — Return Loop

## Release Theme

The first session now ends with a plan instead of a full stop: you choose the days you want to be reminded, and the reminder drops you straight into a review rather than at a dashboard.

## Key Features

**📅 Choose when you come back**
- After your first completed session, NoteLib asks which weekdays you want reminders on, and remembers the answer.
- Board takers confirm their exam date at the same moment; everyone else just picks days, with no date they cannot supply.
- "Not now" is a real answer — the question is asked once and does not come back, on any device.
- Review days are editable later from Email Preferences, so a schedule is a plan rather than a trap.

**⚡ One tap from reminder to first question**
- The due-concepts reminder now opens the note with the most concepts due, already in a review session, instead of leaving you on the dashboard to work out where to go.
- If that note has been deleted or is no longer yours, the link falls back to the dashboard rather than breaking.
- Nothing is sent on a day when nothing is due — a reminder with no content is worse than silence.

## Polish & Fixes
- Reminder frequency is unchanged: you still receive at most one due-concepts digest a week. Choosing days decides *which* day it arrives, not how many arrive.
- Learners who never touch the new setting keep exactly the schedule they already had.
- Reminder days are matched in Philippine time, so "Tuesday" means the same thing for everyone.
- The commitment prompt appears on every screen a first session can end on — Quick Review, Challenge Quiz, Adaptive Practice and Long Exam.
- Declining, or a save that fails, no longer clears review days you had already chosen.
- No new quiz mode, no change to how mastery is calculated, and no change to who receives email by default.
