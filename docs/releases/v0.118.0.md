# Release Notes: v0.118.0 — Note and Study Pack Regeneration

## Release Theme
A note whose source material has moved on can be rewritten — content and Study Pack together — without losing the note's identity, its place in a plan, or your history against it.

## Key Features

**♻️ Regenerate a note and its Study Pack as one thing**
- Regenerating used to rebuild the Study Pack while leaving the note's own content untouched. If the material had genuinely moved on, you had to rewrite the note by hand and regenerate afterwards.
- Choosing **Note + Study Pack** now rewrites both from the note's topic in one operation. The note keeps its id, its creation date, its Study Pack, and its exact place and section in any Study Plan.
- Your existing quiz history is untouched, and any copies other learners made are unaffected.

**🛟 A failed regeneration leaves everything as it was**
- Both pieces are written only after both have been generated successfully. There is no window where a rewritten note sits beside a Study Pack built from the content it replaced.
- If anything fails — the note, the Study Pack, or saving them — the original content and the original Study Pack are both still there, and neither allowance is spent.

**🔀 You choose what gets replaced**
- The Regenerate action now asks: **Study Pack**, or **Note + Study Pack**. Study Pack is the default, every time it opens.
- The combined option shows exactly what it will write from — topic, subject, writing context and depth — with a link to edit those details first.
- Rewriting your own note carries a clear warning that the note's content will be replaced and cannot be brought back, and a public note says plainly that what people see will change while existing copies will not.
- Each option states what it costs before you commit.

**🔒 Your notes can't be edited out from under a regeneration**
- Saving a note while its Study Pack is being generated used to be accepted and then quietly lost when generation finished. It is now declined with a clear message, and nothing you typed is thrown away.

**🎓 A regenerated quiz has to be re-mastered**
- Scoring full marks on a note's Quick Review unlocks the Quiz tab, which shows the answers. After a regeneration that unlock used to carry over to the new questions — so the answer key was open on a quiz you had never sat.
- Mastery now belongs to the quiz it was earned on. Regenerate, and the Quiz tab locks until you score full marks again.

## Polish & Fixes
- The AI metadata suggestion now appears only when a Study Pack is created for the first time. Regenerating never proposes new metadata, because the scope selector already offers to edit those details at the point you are deciding. Bulk generation is unchanged.
- Regenerating a note turns off any live share link for its quiz, so a recipient meets the "no longer active" screen instead of being graded against questions drawn from replaced material. The link is deactivated, never deleted.
- Corrected three documents that described `initiatePool` as refreshing an exam question pool built from replaced content. It does not, and the stale pool is recorded as a known limitation instead.
