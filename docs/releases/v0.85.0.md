# Release Notes: v0.85.0 — Domain Signal Integrity

## Release Theme
Whether your study material gets calculation-heavy questions is now something the subject area states about itself, instead of something the app guessed from its name.

## Key Features

**🧮 Calculation questions follow the subject, not its spelling**
- Every note carries a subject area, and each one now states plainly whether its material involves computation
- Previously the app decided this by looking for words like "engineering" or "accounting" inside the name — so **Accountancy** never matched, because the word it looked for was "accounting"
- Nursing and Accountancy now say so directly, which means dosage calculations, taxation, and financial problems can appear in quizzes and exams generated for them
- Subject areas that are not computational — General Education, Professional Education, Professional Practice & Regulation — say that just as plainly, so they are not pushed toward numbers they do not have
- Notes that have not been assigned a subject area are unaffected: they keep the existing behaviour exactly, so nothing you already have changes on its own

**📖 Every subject area now lists what it covers**
- Choosing a subject area used to mean guessing which one a topic belonged to, with the answer written down nowhere in the app
- Each option now shows its covered subjects at the point of choice — so **Engineering Economics** is visibly filed under Engineering Mathematics, rather than being a question you have to ask someone
- This is the curator authoring screens only; nothing changed for learners

## Polish & Fixes
- Corrected three statements in the knowledge architecture record that no longer described the product: subject areas are not shown on note cards, learners never see that vocabulary, and applicable programs do appear on cards
- Documented how the calculation signal is decided, in both the generation and quiz references, so the old keyword list is no longer mistaken for the rule
- Assessed and declined a proposal to make subject areas freely expandable, on the evidence that the existing eight are barely used — half of them have never been applied to a single note
- Published the read that sized the problem and the worksheet for closing the rest of it
