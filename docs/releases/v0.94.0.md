# Release Notes: v0.94.0 — Connection Experience

## Release Theme

Connecting with someone is now as easy as sending them a link — and being connected still gives away nothing you did not choose to share.

## Key Features

**🔗 Invite someone with a link, not just an email address**

- Create a link from Learning Connections and send it however you like — a message, a chat, in person. You no longer need to know someone's email address to connect with them.
- Each link works **once**. The first person to open it sends you a request, and **you confirm** before the connection exists — so a link that gets forwarded or seen by the wrong person cannot quietly connect them to you.
- You can revoke a link you have not used yet, and a revoked, expired or already-used link simply stops working.
- Someone opening your link keeps their place through signing in **and** through signing up, so a brand-new account still lands on your invitation.

**⏳ Invitations no longer disappear without explanation**

- An emailed invitation now shows how long it has left. When it lapses, it stays in your list and says so, instead of silently vanishing and leaving you to wonder whether the person declined or simply never saw it.
- **Invite again** re-sends it with the address and role already filled in, and reuses the original invitation rather than starting a new one.
- Expired invitations can still be dismissed, and stop cluttering the list after a while on their own.

**🎚️ Turning off "Share my study activity" now actually turns it off**

- Streaks, longest streak, study days and study mode were previously visible through the **progress** view as well, which meant a learner could switch activity sharing off and change nothing a supporter could see. That is fixed: those numbers now require the activity permission, and nothing else.
- A supporter with progress sharing only sees readiness, quiz performance and plan progress — which is exactly what "share my progress" says.

**🗣️ The product stopped promising access it cannot give**

- Several messages still said progress would become available once a connection activated, or that recording guardian consent would unblock it. Neither has been true since progress became its own permission. They now describe the connection's status and nothing more.
- The Help Center covers invitation links, explains that only the learner can share progress, and no longer describes the progress view as showing how often someone studies.

## Polish & Fixes

- A pending invitation now shows its expiry clock, and an expired one is visibly distinct rather than silently absent.
- The dashboard's *People you support* card and the Help Center no longer promise study activity the progress view no longer contains.
- Fixed a case where opening an invitation link on a shared device could route a different person into that invitation after they signed in.
- A connection whose guardian consent is outstanding, or whose age is unknown, no longer shows a progress link that would not work.
- Confirmed by a full pre-release security review that no one can read another person's data without a live permission, that a link cannot create a connection on its own, and that guardian consent cannot be bypassed by using a link instead of an email invitation.
- Added the first tests that exercise the invitation-link database rules against a real database, after a review found several of them could be broken without any test noticing.
- Renamed a test whose name promised a guarantee it did not check, and pointed it at the one that does.
