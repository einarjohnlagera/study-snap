# Release Notes: v0.46.0 — Retention Depth: Due-Concepts Digest & Exam Pacing

## Release Theme

Deepen retention past the conversion-audit-driven fixes of recent releases: a new out-of-app weekly digest that pulls learners back when concepts go due, an owned-content pacing plan for Board Exam takers counting down to their exam date, and a round of app-feel polish that makes everyday study sessions feel snappier and less cluttered.

## Key Features

**📬 Weekly Due-Concepts Email Digest**
- Learners with due concepts across their notes now get a weekly email summarizing what's due and linking back into the app to review.
- Opt-out is available from Settings alongside the app's other email preferences, independent of other reminder types.

**🎯 Board Exam Dashboard Pacing**
- Board Exam profiles with an exam date set now see a daily concept pacing target on the Dashboard — how many due concepts to clear per day to stay on track before the exam.
- Falls back to the existing exam countdown when a pacing target can't be computed (no due concepts, exam date already passed, etc.).

**📈 Session-Completion Pacing Echo**
- Finishing a Quick Review, Challenge Quiz, or Adaptive Practice session tied to a weekly-goal collection now echoes how many weeks remain on that goal's countdown, reinforcing the connection between today's session and the broader plan.

## Polish & Fixes

- Note cards across Dashboard and public discovery surfaces now show consistent press feedback on tap/click.
- Note Detail shows a proper loading skeleton while the note is first fetched, instead of plain loading text.
- Note Detail's Recent Sessions and Performance Overview sections now collapse by default once there's real history to show, keeping the page shorter and more scannable — expand either with a single tap.

## Known Limitations

None specific to this release.
