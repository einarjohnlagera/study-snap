# Release Notes: v0.72.1 — Constraint Check

## Release Theme

Nothing changes for learners in this release: it went looking for which constraint on the product is real, found that the number we had been steering by was measuring a quarter of what it claimed, and fixed the instrument.

## Key Features

**📊 Retention is now reported over four windows, not one**
- The admin funnel showed a single "returned in week 2" rate covering days 7–14 after a learner's first Study Pack.
- A production read found that window sees 3 of the 11 learners who actually came back — it misses everyone returning in days 2–7, and everyone returning after day 14.
- Four readings now sit side by side: strict days 7–14, after day 7, days 2–30, and after day 1, each showing the number of users it is calculated from.
- The weekly cohort table gains an after-day-7 column beside the strict one, so the two definitions can be compared week by week.

**🔒 The historical number is preserved exactly**
- The strict days 7–14 figure keeps its definition and its denominator unchanged, so the rate quoted for months stays comparable.
- The wider readings use their own, separately labelled set of learners, and the page explains why the two differ.
- This means a change in the number can only mean a change in the product — never a change in how it was counted.

## Polish & Fixes
- Unbounded windows grow as accounts age, so the page warns they are not comparable across cohorts of different ages and points to the weekly table for that.
- A dataset too young to fill the wider windows shows zeros against its own denominator, rather than hiding the section or borrowing the narrow figure.
- The activation read that opened this release is recorded in full, including the two corrections found in its own queries on first run.
- The measurement obligation attached to the previous release's return-loop work was re-aimed: it had been pointed at the narrow window and would have judged that work by an instrument missing most returns.
- No learner-facing behaviour changed anywhere — no quiz mode, no email, no page a learner can reach.
