# Release Notes: v0.95.0 — Redemption Integrity

## Release Theme
Opening someone's invitation link should not cost you anything permanent before either of you has agreed to anything.

## Key Features

**🔐 A birth year stays provisional until the connection is confirmed**
- Redeeming an invitation link no longer writes your account-level birth year
- Your declared year is held against that pending request only, and becomes permanent when the other person confirms
- If they never confirm, or either of you cancels, it is deleted and nothing is left on your account
- Guardian consent still works throughout, so a minor is protected before anything is written

**🔗 Link surfaces show what is actually true**
- The live-links list refreshes after you create or revoke a link, and when you return to the page
- Copy and Revoke now act on a recent server response instead of a page-load snapshot
- Reloading after you have sent a request no longer looks like a broken link

**👤 A redeemed link tells the creator to act**
- When someone opens your link, you are now told that you are the one who confirms it
- Previously the connection simply showed as "not active yet", naming nobody

**♿ Connection controls are reachable by keyboard**
- A sharing switch that is temporarily unavailable stays in the tab order and announces why
- Birth-year inputs, their steppers and the guardian-consent checkbox follow standard labelling

## Polish & Fixes
- A pending connection no longer says existing sharing choices will "resume" — on a request that was never accepted, nothing had started
- Withdrawing an invitation reports what the server actually did, instead of assuming it succeeded, and refreshes your connections alongside your invitations
- Invitation-link feedback appears inside the link card rather than two cards further down the page
- The "Quiz for someone" dialog now states that questions and answers are AI-generated and worth reviewing before you share them, and says so even when quota information fails to load
- Guardian consent, acceptance and both parties' connection lists all read one effective birth year, with the account value always taking precedence
- A connection list no longer takes a database write lock on every person in it
- The provisional birth-year lookup is now structurally tied to its own relationship, so a mismatched request cannot reach another person's declared year

## Known limitations
- An unconfirmed connection request never expires. Invitation links and email invitations both lapse after 30 days, but the pending request a redemption creates does not — either person can cancel it, it is visible in both connection lists, and deleting an account removes it.
- A provisional birth year does not yet appear in the account data export. Including it has been decided; it is not built.
- The rule that an accepted connection always carries a promoted birth year holds by design and its parts are tested, but no end-to-end two-thread test exercises it against real rows.
