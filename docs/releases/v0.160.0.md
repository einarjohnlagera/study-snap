# Release Notes: v0.160.0 — Study Plans by Semester

## Release Theme

A curator can place each Subject Plan of a Year in an academic term, so a multi-semester curriculum reads as a semester-by-semester study plan, without adding a level to the collection hierarchy and without touching any Note.

## Key Features

**🗓️ Study plans grouped by semester**
- A Year whose Subject Plans carry a term now shows static term headers, in term order, each with a subject count and an in-progress count once anything is started.
- Every Subject shows as a compact card: title, note count, and one progress signal (`N% ready` or `Not started`).
- Term grouping and compact cards turn on together, from one condition: any Subject having a term. A Year with no terms, including all five live Review Sets, renders exactly as before.
- The dashboard's current step and the Continue action follow the order you see on screen.

**🧭 Curators assign terms in the Year builder**
- Each Subject row has a `Term` field that suggests the terms already used in that Year and accepts a new one. The curator never types an order: an existing term reuses its order, a new term takes the next one.
- A live warning appears while a Year is only partly termed, and the Year cannot be published (or receive a Publish update) until every Subject has a term or none does.
- Once a Subject Plan is published its term is fixed, and the field shows that. An adopted copy never edits a term, and the control is hidden there.

**📦 Terms survive adoption and updates**
- A term is carried when a learner adopts a Year, when they adopt a whole Goal, when an already-owned Subject is attached to an adopted Goal, and when a later Official update adds a new Subject.
- Official update stays additive-only: it never rewrites a learner's existing Subjects.

**🛠️ Curriculum pipeline understands terms**
- The plan file accepts an optional `academic_term` column, checked per Study Plan: every Subject Plan has a term or none does, and a partial file is refused with the Study Plan and the unassigned Subject Plans named.
- The term order is derived from the sequence in the file and printed in the workbook. A file without terms builds exactly the workbook it always did.
- The strategist module's TSV header now includes the `applicable_programs` column the builder has required since September, which it had drifted from.

**📐 A decision record for hierarchy depth**
- ADR-003 records that collections have exactly two persisted levels, that a curriculum label such as a term is placement and not a level, that placement never lives on the Note, and that a degree carries no learner progress.

## Polish & Fixes
- The frontend and backend can deploy in either order: the new request fields are optional, the new response fields are nullable, and the migration only adds two nullable columns.
- Retroactively adding or renaming terms on a Year that is already published is not supported in this release; the five live Review Sets can therefore not gain terms. It is logged as a future release rather than left as an open limitation.
- Moving a published Subject that carries a term, or into a termed Year, is refused, because it would leave that Subject without a term and permanently locked.
- The pipeline's label length check counts the same units as the backend, so a label the pipeline accepts is not rejected on save.
- A stored term literally named `Term not specified` no longer produces a duplicate React key on the Year page.
- Two pre-signoff cold reviews found and fixed a Continue target that could differ from the first card shown, a publish error that could name a Subject the curator cannot change, and a move that could strand a Subject; the remaining edge cases are written down in `RELEASES.md`.
