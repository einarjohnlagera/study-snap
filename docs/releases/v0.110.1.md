# Release Notes: v0.110.1 — Quiz Text Integrity

## Release Theme

A quiz choice now says exactly what its author wrote, and a quiz you built for someone stays findable after you close the tab.

## Key Features

**✏️ Quiz answer choices stop losing characters**

- Choices that begin with a letter and a dot — `D.C. generator`, `A.C. motor`, `C. elegans`, `B. Smith` — were quietly losing their first few characters, and lost a few more every time the quiz was opened
- Fixed everywhere a quiz is read, copied or re-used: study sessions, shared quizzes, copied notes, and second exam versions
- Newly generated quizzes are unaffected and were always correct

**🔎 Find a quiz you made for someone**

- Combined quizzes now have a list in your Library, so one you built earlier is reachable again
- From there you can open it, copy its link, or turn sharing off — previously the page was only reachable in the moment you created it, so closing the tab left a live link you could not switch off

## Polish & Fixes

- Each quiz in the list shows when you made it, how many sections and questions it holds, and whether it is currently shared
- Re-copying a shared link no longer costs you another share link from your monthly allowance
