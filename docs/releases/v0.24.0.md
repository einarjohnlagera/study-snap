# Release Notes: v0.24.0 — Guided Learning

## Release Theme
"Guided Learning" — NoteLib now closes the study loop. Every quiz mode feeds your concept mastery data, result screens suggest your actual next study step, and a new Progress report shows you exactly where you stand across all your subjects.

## Key Features

**📊 My Progress Report**
- New `/progress` page shows mastered, due, and not-yet-practiced concept counts per subject across all your Study Packs
- Dashboard Focus Areas card links directly to your full report
- See what's due for spaced-repetition review at a glance, not just what you got wrong last session

**🔁 Concept-Driven Next Steps After Every Quiz**
- Quick Review, Challenge Quiz, Board Exam Mode, and Adaptive Practice now all feed your concept mastery spine on completion
- Result screens resolve your next action from real spaced-repetition signals — which concepts are due, which are weak — instead of client-side wrong-answer lists
- The learning loop is fully closed: practice → assess → see gaps → targeted next step

**🆓 Adaptive Practice for Free (3 sessions/month)**
- Free users now get 3 Adaptive Practice sessions per month
- Removes the paywall that blocked weak-area follow-up after a Quick Review or Challenge Quiz — the loop now works on every plan

**📋 Full Study Pack Copying**
- Copying a public note now includes the generated Study Pack — summary, key concepts, and quiz arrive ready to use
- A "Copy note only" option is available if you prefer to start fresh
- Copied note arrives as Study Pack Ready when the source has a Study Pack

**🔄 Study Pack Regeneration**
- Regenerate your Study Pack any time your notes change — the updated pack replaces the old one in-place so your session history and progress stay linked
- Note content is editable again; regeneration requires explicit confirmation
