# Release Notes: v0.73.0 — Onboarding Redesign

## Release Theme

Setting up NoteLib now feels like telling it what you're here to learn, rather than filling in a profile before you're allowed in.

## Key Features

**🪜 One question per screen**
- Onboarding used to ask three things at once on one screen and two on another. Now each screen asks a single question and gets out of the way.
- Choosing a profile or a starting point moves you on immediately; screens where you type something wait for you to say you're done.
- Back works everywhere it should, and returning to a screen you already answered never leaves you stuck.

**🎯 Fewer things to answer**
- Your learning level is filled in from the profile you picked — exam reviewers start on board review level, students on college — so most learners can simply carry on.
- Every question is now phrased for who you said you are: teachers are asked what they teach, exam reviewers what they're reviewing for, professionals what field they're in.
- Each screen says what your answer actually changes, in one line, instead of a paragraph of reassurance.

**🙋 Ask for a study plan that doesn't exist yet**
- If there's no Official Study Plan for your course or program, you can now register that you want one, instead of only being told it isn't there.
- Asking doesn't interrupt anything — you stay where you are and can still start with your own notes or browse what other learners have shared.

**📊 We can finally see where people give up**
- The admin dashboard now shows how far learners get through onboarding, screen by screen, and which study plans have been requested most.
- This is the instrument for judging whether this redesign actually worked.

## Polish & Fixes
- The "no plan yet for your program" screen leads with what you *can* do today, and its buttons say where they take you — "Continue" if you're staying, "Finish" if you're heading to the public library.
- The last step of the ready-made path used to jump the step counter to the end; it now shows where you really are.
- Selecting an option now visibly highlights it — on two screens it silently didn't.
- Option cards stack the same way on every screen, so the layout doesn't shift as you move through.
- Headings are smaller and calmer, and duplicated helper text is gone.
- A learner part-way through onboarding when this release lands keeps their answers and resumes on the right screen.
- Nothing in onboarding drops you straight into a quiz — you always choose what to do with your first Study Pack.
- Exam takers are asked for their exam date here and again after their first session — kept on purpose, because the later prompt only appears once you finish a session, and the countdown to exam day should work for everyone who tells us the date. The second ask is pre-filled with whatever you gave here.
