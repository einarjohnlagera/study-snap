# Release Notes: v0.90.0 — Invitation Integrity

## Release Theme
Inviting someone no longer reveals whether they have an account — and you can now invite someone who has never signed up.

## Key Features

**✉️ Invite anyone, by email**
- An invitation is now stored against the address you type, not against an account
- **You can invite someone who has not signed up yet** — they follow the link, create an account, and find the invitation waiting
- Inviting an address that has no account and one that does now look identical, so nobody can use the invite box to test whether an email belongs to a NoteLib user

**⏳ Invitations don't wait forever**
- An invitation is an open offer to whoever controls that address, so it now expires after 30 days
- Re-inviting a lapsed address simply reissues it, keeping the date you first reached out
- Sending is metered, so nobody can be repeatedly mailed through someone else's invite box

**🔒 Proving you own the address is the point**
- Accepting an invitation, or anything that grants access, now requires a verified email
- **Ending a connection and correcting your own birth year deliberately stay open** — they cut access rather than granting it, and blocking them would hurt the person the check protects

**🎯 The right place for "Quiz for someone"**
- Moved out of the Study Pack practice row into the note actions menu, where the other sharing actions live
- It is a way to help someone else, not a way to practise — beside *Start Quick Review* it read as an alternative to studying
- **Still available to everyone**, since whoever receives a shared quiz needs no account and no connection

## Polish & Fixes
- Connection cards no longer show a blank space where a name should be
- The Dashboard now says what a pending connection is actually waiting on, instead of claiming an invitation still needs accepting when it was already accepted
- Fixed a case where accepting a connection could complete on an out-of-date birth year, leaving a young learner connected without the guardian confirmation the step exists to collect
- Fixed a case where ending a connection could be undone by an acceptance already in progress
- Re-sending a lapsed invitation in the other direction now uses the direction you just chose, rather than reviving the original one
- The supporter progress page has a proper back link
- Supporter progress now also requires a verified email, as a second line of defence
- Fixed the birth-year correction card being hidden after you invite someone — you can now fix a mistyped year straight away, instead of waiting for the other person to accept
- The message shown after sending an invitation no longer says the other person needs an account
