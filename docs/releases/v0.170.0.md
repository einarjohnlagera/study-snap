# Release Notes: v0.170.0 — Study Journey, Closed

## Release Theme

NoteLib's Study Journey architecture closes out: learners can now discover an official Review Set
straight from a public note, and exam-bound learners can hold more than one exam goal at a time.

## Key Features

**🔗 Public Note → Official Review Set**
- A public note's detail page now links straight to its official Review Set, when that note belongs
  to one — a top-level, curator-owned set, not just any public collection.
- The link reuses the existing signed-out discovery flow, so a visitor who isn't logged in yet can
  still follow it and land back where they started after signing up.

**🎯 Multiple exam goals**
- Profile's Study Focus now lets a learner adopt several exam goals (ALE, PNLE, LET, CPALE, CELE) at
  once, mark one active, and switch or remove without losing any adopted study material or practice
  history.
- The dashboard and goal nudges follow the active goal; a post-session next step still points at
  whichever adopted goal actually matches the note just studied, even if that isn't the active one.
- Subject focus and exam goals stay mutually exclusive, as before — Profile now asks for confirmation
  before either choice clears the other, in both directions.
- Fixed two confirmed-live bugs along the way: the goal-prompt banner no longer ignores an existing
  subject focus (it used to silently wipe it with one click), and its "no thanks" dismissal now
  actually persists across reloads.
- Legacy single-goal accounts migrate automatically and losslessly into the new model; nothing for
  existing exam-goal learners to redo.

## Polish & Fixes
- Account data export now includes every adopted exam goal, not just the active one.
- Both items shipped ahead of their own evidence gates by explicit owner decision — multi-goal's
  usage population hadn't grown past the "worth building" threshold the owner set in `v0.167.0`, and
  the public-note link shipped a day ahead of a production-stability checkpoint clearing. Both now
  carry their own post-ship checkpoints in `ROADMAP.md` to read whether the override paid off.
- A pre-signoff review found no blocking defects; a handful of minor, non-live edge cases (a
  whitespace gap in the migration's slug matching, a brief write-ordering window during deploy
  rollover) are recorded as known limitations rather than silently dropped.
