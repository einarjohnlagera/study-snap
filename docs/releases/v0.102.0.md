# Release Notes: v0.102.0 — Plan-Sourced Assessment

## Release Theme

The Study Plan you build can finally be the thing you are tested on — a capability the product appeared to offer and actually refused.

## Key Features

**📚 A Study Plan can now source a multi-note exam**
- Open a plan, press its exam button, and the exam is built from the notes in that plan
- Until now this looked like it worked and then failed at the last step: the screen pre-selected your plan's notes for you, and starting the exam returned *"source notes must be owned by you and share the same subject"* — the product refusing a selection it had made itself
- What decides which notes are eligible is now **membership of the plan**, not whether their Subject text happens to match. Two notes you deliberately filed together are no longer rejected because one says *Engineering Mathematics* and the other says *Engineering Math*

**📐 The note limit is honest about your level**
- The real limit was never a flat number: it depends on how many questions your learner level generates, so it is 6, 8 or 10 notes rather than the 4 offered before
- The prestart screen now says what will actually be covered — *"Testing material from 5 of 12 Notes in this plan"* — and tells you when notes are excluded because they have no Study Pack yet
- The limit comes from the server rather than being guessed at in the browser, so what you are shown and what the exam enforces cannot drift apart

**🎓 Board Exam plans work too**
- Board Exam is the plan action for board-exam learners, and it had exactly the same fault
- It was almost missed: a review challenged an assumption that it "already worked", and checking found it did not. Fixing only the other path would have left the feature broken for most of the people it was built for
- Board Exam keeps its existing two-note limit; changing it interacts with how its questions are counted and is a separate decision

**📊 Finishing an exam is now recorded**
- Board Exam and Challenge Quiz completions are captured for the first time. Both had a recorded start and no recorded finish, so there was no way to tell whether people were finishing what they began
- Exam starts also record how many notes were used and whether they came from a plan

## Polish & Fixes

- The Study Plans help guide no longer says nothing is generated for a plan, which this release made untrue
- A pre-release review found the new permission check had no real test behind it: removing it entirely left all 1894 tests passing. It is now covered directly, and the check that was missing is verified by deliberately breaking it
- Corrected the recorded note count on completed exams, which reported zero for any single-note session
- The record of whether an exam came from a plan now reflects what the server verified rather than what the request asked for
- A selection limit is never shown to someone whose plan cannot start an exam, rather than showing a number the server never sanctioned
