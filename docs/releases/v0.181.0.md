# Release Notes: v0.181.0 — Affordable NoteLib

## Release Theme
Every price a learner sees on NoteLib now comes from the same source that decides what they are charged, so when the Philippine prices are lowered (Plus ₱99, Pro ₱149) every page and every checkout changes together.

## Key Features

**🏠 Homepage Prices Read From the Backend**
- The homepage pricing section asks the public pricing service for the visitor's own price and shows the plain monthly amount (for example `₱179 / 1 month`), in the visitor's region and currency.
- The homepage never shows an introductory price, because the page cannot tell a new subscriber from a returning one.
- While the price loads, a same-height placeholder holds the space, so the page does not jump when the number arrives. If the price cannot be loaded, the card shows a calm "See current prices" link to the pricing page, and never an amount.
- The homepage is still prepared in advance and served as a static page.

**💳 One Source for Every Price**
- The plan cards on the pricing page, the paywall and Settings no longer fall back to numbers stored in the website while the real price loads or fails: they show a placeholder while loading and the existing "pricing unavailable" wording if the price cannot be fetched.
- An intro offer appears only when the backend says the viewer is eligible for it. A successful response that says "not eligible" no longer shows an old intro hint (this fixes the long-standing fallback bug).
- The static "Regional Pricing" card on the pricing page is removed, and the stored price and intro tables are deleted from the website's configuration, with a test that fails the build if a peso amount or one of those tables comes back.

**🧪 Stronger Safety Net**
- Tests on the pricing page, paywall and Settings now fail if any currency amount appears when pricing cannot be loaded, and the plan cards are tested with the real loading hook through loading, success and failure.

## Polish & Fixes
- This is a **code sign-off only**: the release is built, verified and approved as a release candidate. Nothing is deployed, and no production merge or deployment is authorized by this sign-off; the deployment is gated on the previous release's stabilization read.
- The price change itself is **not code**: Plus ₱99 and Pro ₱149 in the Philippines, with Pro yearly and the exam pass hidden, is the owner's own Render environment save at least 24 hours after the frontend deployment, followed by the owner's real ₱99 test purchase.
- Verified with the full frontend suite (244 suites, 2,943 tests), a clean type check and lint, the build's route table (`/` and `/pricing` still static), eleven mutation checks each caught by a named test, one scoped cold review of ten claims, and a local production build driven in a real headless browser against a stand-in pricing service: every state, no layout shift, and exactly one pricing request per page load.
- In Xendit test mode, the hosted checkout page was confirmed to show the exact amount, the currency and the plan description ("PHP 149.00", "NoteLib Plus Monthly - Intro offer applied") before any payment method is chosen.
- Known and accepted: until the owner's environment save, a new Philippine subscriber is still charged the live intro price at checkout, so the homepage's plain price can be higher than what they pay; this ends with the environment save.
- Known and accepted: a returning subscriber whose sign-in has expired is treated as signed out and can see an intro they will not be charged on `/pricing`, the paywall and Settings (the same behaviour as before this release).
- Known, owner-accepted risk: every browser visit to the homepage now makes one pricing request (up to six small reads on the backend, uncached); the first-hour monitoring criteria (roll back the frontend on a non-deploy database kill, sustained errors for the pricing request, a sustained call rate far above normal, or database connections above the level seen before) were approved by the owner on 2026-11-12.
- Known limitations, documented and not fixed: a pricing request that never finishes keeps the placeholder with no timeout; the placeholders are hidden from screen readers; when pricing fails, checkout buttons stay enabled and the hosted checkout shows the exact amount; currency formatting follows the browser's locale.
- No backend code, migration, voucher, entitlement, quota or checkout change; `backend/pom.xml` stays at `0.180.0` because this is a frontend-only release.
