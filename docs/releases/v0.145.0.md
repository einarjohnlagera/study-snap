# Release Notes: v0.145.0 — Knowledge, Not Role

## Release Theme

Teaches the LLM authoring pipeline that a professional role belongs to *who reads* a note, not to
the biomedical mechanism itself — closing a live mis-instruction on six production notes currently
generated under `Domain: Nursing` with no nursing content at all.

## Key Features

**🧬 Basic Medical Sciences Domain Context**
- A new authoring domain, `Basic Medical Sciences`, joins the Domain Context taxonomy for
  biomedical knowledge shared across health professions — drug action and therapeutic mechanisms,
  pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics, disease mechanisms and pathophysiology, and shared
  laboratory or diagnostic parameters.
- The boundary: does a professional role appear in the knowledge itself, or only in who is reading
  it? A note whose subject is the nursing role itself — medication administration rights,
  assessment and monitoring duties, prioritization — stays `Nursing`. A note teaching mechanism or
  pathophysiology with no professional role embedded in it belongs here instead.
- Six production Pharmacology notes (including `Antibiotics: Mechanism of Action and Resistance`)
  were being generated under `Domain: Nursing` despite containing no nursing content — an active
  mis-instruction this value now lets a curator correct.
- The quantitative-computation keyword scan also gained one entry, `"pharmacokinetic"`, closing a
  gap where genuinely computational pharmacokinetics material (half-life, clearance, dosing) had
  been receiving no computation guidance at the fastest-checked generation tier.

## Polish & Fixes
- `ADR-001`'s architecture record gained a new decision entry and two corrected stale figures
  (Domain Context usage counts and the current course-catalog size).
- No database migration, no backfill, no resolver rewrite — the new value is purely additive, and
  reclassifying existing notes is curator content work outside this release.
