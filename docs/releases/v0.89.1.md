# Release Notes: v0.89.1 — Birth Year Correction

## Release Theme
A learner who mis-declared their age can fix it, and the guardian consent step holds when they do.

## Key Features

**🎂 Correct your birth year**
- The year you gave when forming a learning connection can now be corrected from Learning connections
- Previously it was permanent — a wrong year, or one entered under pressure, silently switched off the guardian confirmation step for every future connection you would ever make
- Before you confirm, you are told exactly how many active connections will pause

**🛡️ The consent step re-applies itself**
- Correcting to a younger age pauses any active connection that never had a guardian's confirmation, until one is given
- Connections that already have a guardian's confirmation are unaffected — a confirmation already given is not taken back
- A paused connection is not ended: both people keep the connection they agreed to, and it resumes once confirmation is recorded
- Both sides are told why a connection paused and what will resume it

## Polish & Fixes
- Correcting to an older age changes nothing, and re-entering the same year does nothing at all
- Your birth year is still only ever asked for when you form a connection — never at sign-up, during onboarding, or in your profile
- Only the current year and when it last changed are kept; no history of what was entered before
- Documentation now covers the People you support card, the Learning connections page and menu item, what a data export contains, and what account deletion removes
