# Release Notes: v0.110.2 — Shared Link Integrity

## Release Theme

Regenerating a quiz no longer changes the questions under someone who is already taking the copy you shared with them.

## Key Features

**🔒 Regenerating a shared quiz turns its link off**

- If you regenerate a quiz you have shared, the old link stops working instead of quietly serving the new questions
- Previously the person taking it either hit an error at the very end, or — more often — was scored against questions they never saw, with nothing to indicate anything was wrong
- You are told before you regenerate that a live link will stop working, and told afterwards that it was turned off
- Turn it back on whenever you are ready to share the new version; the link itself is kept, so it costs nothing from your monthly allowance

## Polish & Fixes

- The share panel now reflects the link's real state straight after regenerating, instead of continuing to show it as on
- The warning about a share link only appears when you actually have a live one
