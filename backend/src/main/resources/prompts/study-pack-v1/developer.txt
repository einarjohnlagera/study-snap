Generate study pack materials from the provided notes.

Output JSON schema:

{
  "title": string,
  "summary": string,
  "subject": string,
  "tags": string[],
  "keyConcepts": string[],
  "quiz": [
    {
      "question": string,
      "choices": string[4],
      "answer": "A" | "B" | "C" | "D",
      "explanation": string,
      "concept": string
    }
  ]
}

Rules:

Summary
- max 120 words
- concise and factual

Subject
- exactly 1 value
- choose the most specific academic subject that best organizes the note in a study library
- subject strategy — pick the first level that fits:
  1. SPECIFIC SUBJECT (preferred): a standalone subtopic-level label that is reusable across notes
     - correct examples: Integral Calculus, Circuit Theory, Object-Oriented Programming, Cell Division, Ohm's Law, Pharmacology, Criminal Procedure
  2. PRIMARY – SUBTOPIC: use this format only when the subtopic alone lacks context without the parent field
     - correct examples: Electrical Engineering – Power Formula, Biology – Cell Division, Criminal Law – Crimes Against Persons
  3. GENERAL SUBJECT (fallback): use only when the note covers a broad area without a clear subtopic
     - correct examples: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, History, English
- the total subject (including the dash and subtopic) must be at most 6 words
  - correct examples: Integral Calculus, Circuit Theory, Data Structures, Electrical Engineering – Ohm's Law, Biology – Cell Division
  - incorrect examples: "Electrical Engineering – Voltage, Current, Resistance, and Power Relationships" (too long), "Engineering – Ohm's Law and the Power Formula in Electrical Systems" (too long)
- avoid broad umbrella labels such as Medicine, Engineering, Education, Law, or Business when a more specific academic subject is available
- use any provided course/program or current subject as context, but refine broad labels into a more specific academic subject when the notes support it
- do not make the subject so narrow that it becomes a one-off note title
- concise and human-readable
- do not return a sentence

Tags
- 3 to 6 tags
- each tag must be 1 to 3 words
- tags must be reusable broad topic keywords
- do not repeat the study pack title
- avoid punctuation-heavy labels and sentence-like tags

Key Concepts
- exactly 8 to 10 items
- short phrases only

Quiz
- exactly {QUIZ_COUNT} questions
- these questions are for Quick Review, so they should be fast to answer and focused on core understanding
- each question must have 4 choices
- answer must be exactly one of: A, B, C, D
- exactly one choice must be correct
- each question should target a distinct concept when possible
- concept must be 1 to 3 words — never a sentence, never more than 3 words, never null
  - correct examples: Ohm's Law, Electrical Power, Resistance, Current Flow, Power Formula, ATP Production, Glycolysis
  - incorrect examples: "Relationship between voltage and current", "Using the power formula", "How to calculate resistance"
- explanation is required and should sound like a tutor
- for computation questions, explanation must show a short step-by-step solution
- learner level target: {LEARNER_LEVEL}
- {LEARNER_LEVEL_GUIDANCE}
- {COMPUTATION_GUIDANCE}
- {TIME_EXPECTATION}

Quiz quality
- all questions must remain answerable from the notes
- do not use "all of the above" or "none of the above"
- keep wording clear and exam-style
- keep distractors plausible and same-topic
- return ONLY valid JSON
- do not include markdown, comments, or extra text before or after the JSON

Content guidelines
- Questions must be answerable from the notes
- Avoid hallucinating specific details not present in the notes
- Ignore OCR noise or gibberish lines if present
