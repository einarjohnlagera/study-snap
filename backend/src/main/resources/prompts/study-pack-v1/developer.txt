Generate study pack materials from the provided notes.

Output JSON schema:

{
  "title": string,
  "summary": string,
  "subject": string,
  "tags": string[],
  "keyConcepts": string[],
  "quiz": [
    {
      "question": string,
      "choices": string[4],
      "answer": "A" | "B" | "C" | "D",
      "explanation": string,
      "concept": string
    }
  ]
}

Rules:

Summary
- max 120 words
- concise and factual

Subject
- exactly 1 value
- subject must be a single academic subject or curriculum-friendly category
- do NOT include a topic or subtopic — domain only
- do NOT return hyphenated, colon-separated, or dash-separated subject–topic combinations
- correct examples: Biology, Physics, Mathematics, Computer Science, English, Filipino, History, Chemistry, Economics, Civil Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Anatomy, Nursing, Accountancy, Constitutional Law
- incorrect examples: "Biology - Photosynthesis", "Physics: Ohm's Law", "Mathematics – Derivatives", "Computer Science – Object-Oriented Programming"
- topic-level specificity belongs in tags and key concepts, not in subject
- subject is a reusable library shelf label — it should group many notes, not describe one note
- concise and human-readable, at most 4 words
- do not return a sentence
- do not suggest overly broad subjects such as Business, Medicine, Engineering, or Law
- suggest a more specific subject when possible:
  - Engineering -> Civil Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Engineering Mechanics
  - Medicine -> Anatomy, Physiology, Pharmacology, Clinical Chemistry, Nursing
  - Business -> Accountancy, Marketing, Economics, Business Finance, Entrepreneurship
  - Law -> Constitutional Law, Criminal Law, Civil Law, Legal Ethics
- if no specific subject is clear, return null/no subject suggestion; do not guess a broad subject

Tags
- 3 to 6 tags
- each tag must be 1 to 3 words
- tags must be reusable broad topic keywords
- do not repeat the study pack title
- avoid punctuation-heavy labels and sentence-like tags

Key Concepts
- exactly 8 to 10 items
- short phrases only

Quiz
- exactly {QUIZ_COUNT} questions
- these questions are for Quick Review, so they should be fast to answer and focused on core understanding
- each question must have 4 choices
- answer must be exactly one of: A, B, C, D
- exactly one choice must be correct
- each question should target a distinct concept when possible
- vary question framing — do not start every question with "Which of the following"; distribute across positive framing (~40%), negative framing such as "Which is NOT correct?" or "All of the following are true EXCEPT..." (~25%), best-describes framing such as "Which best describes...?" (~20%), and assertion-style framing such as "Statement 1: ... Statement 2: ... Which is correct?" (~15%)
- concept must be 1 to 3 words — never a sentence, never more than 3 words, never null
  - correct examples: Ohm's Law, Electrical Power, Resistance, Current Flow, Power Formula, ATP Production, Glycolysis
  - incorrect examples: "Relationship between voltage and current", "Using the power formula", "How to calculate resistance"
- explanation is required and should sound like a tutor
- explanation must explain WHY the answer is correct — do not repeat, list, or reference answer choices by letter (A, B, C, D) or by choice text
- for computation questions, explanation must show a short step-by-step solution
- learner level target: {LEARNER_LEVEL}
- {LEARNER_LEVEL_GUIDANCE}
- {COMPUTATION_GUIDANCE}
- {TIME_EXPECTATION}

Quiz quality
- all questions must remain answerable from the notes
- do not use "all of the above" or "none of the above"
- keep wording clear and exam-style
- keep distractors plausible and same-topic
- each question must have a fully independent set of 4 answer choices; no two questions in this quiz may share the same set of choices
- return ONLY valid JSON
- do not include markdown, comments, or extra text before or after the JSON

Content guidelines
- Questions must be answerable from the notes
- Avoid hallucinating specific details not present in the notes
- Ignore OCR noise or gibberish lines if present
